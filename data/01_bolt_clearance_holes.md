# Metric Bolt Clearance Hole Sizes (based on ISO 273)

Clearance holes are the holes a bolt passes through without threading.
All values are hole diameters in millimeters.

## Fit classes
- **Fine**: tight fit, used when precise alignment is required.
- **Medium**: general-purpose fit. **This is the company default** when no fit class is specified.
- **Coarse**: loose fit, used for easy assembly or large tolerances.

## Usage notes
- "Standard clearance hole for M6" means the medium fit: 6.6 mm diameter.
- "Hole for an M10 bolt" without further detail means a medium clearance hole: 11.0 mm diameter.
- Radius = diameter / 2. For example, an M8 medium hole has radius 4.5 mm.
