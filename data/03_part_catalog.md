# Part Catalog: Plates and Brackets

All dimensions in mm. Parts are centered at the origin unless stated otherwise.
Hole positions are given as (x, y) coordinates of the hole center.

## MP-200 — Standard Mounting Plate (large)
- Shape: square plate
- Size: 200 × 200 mm
- Corner fillet radius: 10 mm
- Holes: 4 corner holes
  - Diameter: 9.0 mm (M8 medium clearance)
  - Offset from each edge: 20 mm
  - Positions: (−80, −80), (80, −80), (80, 80), (−80, 80)
